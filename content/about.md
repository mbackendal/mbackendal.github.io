I am an assistant professor at [USI](https://www.usi.ch/en) (Università della Svizzera italiana) where I do research in applied cryptography.
Before I joined USI, I was a PhD student at ETH Zurich, advised by [Kenny Paterson](https://appliedcrypto.ethz.ch/people/person-detail.MjU0MDM1.TGlzdC8zMzE4LC0yODgzMDgzMDc=.html).
I wrote my [doctoral thesis](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/entities/publication/e5006f0e-3556-4562-ab3f-1389503a07af) on provable security for applied cryptography, focused on cloud storage security [[1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/989), [2](https://mega-awry.io/), [3](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/546)] and key derivation [[4](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/657), [5](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/935), [6](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/861)].

I am a co-founder and organizer of [CAW](https://caw.cryptanalysis.fun/), the Cryptographic Applications Workshop.

Before my PhD, I studied engineering mathematics at Lunds Tekniska Högskola (LTH) in Sweden. I also worked with [Mihir Bellare](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~mihir/) during an exchange at UCSD. I wrote [my master thesis](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/search/publication/8979963) on puncturable symmetric key encapsulation mechanisms, supervised by [Felix Günther](https://www.felixguenther.info/).

In my free time, I like climbing and hiking in the beautiful mountains of Switzerland, trying new foods and learning languages, among other things!
